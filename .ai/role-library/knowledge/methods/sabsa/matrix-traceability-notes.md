# SABSA matrix and traceability evidence

Use this reference when linking contextual and conceptual artefacts or
checking where an architectural claim lacks a business basis.

Start with the source business driver and accountable owner. Connect the
applicable business attribute and its measurement basis to the downstream
control objective. Keep stable identifiers and evidence locators for each
claimed relation. Where several drivers or attributes apply, record each
relation and its scope. Where a link is missing or contested, record the
gap and refer it to the accountable layer role.

Use the matrix dimension and viewpoint that fit the question. This note
applies to traceability and leaves role-specific deliverables, document
ownership and review in the relevant professional and workflow contracts.
