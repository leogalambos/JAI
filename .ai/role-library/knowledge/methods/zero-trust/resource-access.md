# Zero Trust resource access and transition

Resource access design, migration or independent assessment with a versioned scoped baseline.

## Inventory

Version the subject, workload and device identities, resources, observed flows, dependencies and accountable owners. Record source, observation window, confidence and coverage gaps. Derive measurable access and availability criteria from attributable requirements.

[SP 800-207 Zero Trust Architecture](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final)

## Decisions

Document subject and resource identity, device or workload posture, context signals and trust assumptions for each access decision. Map each policy decision to an enforcement point. Specify expected allow, deny and unavailable-signal outcomes and evidence for each.

[SP 800-207 Zero Trust Architecture](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final)
