# Red Hat Enterprise Linux enterprise lifecycle evidence

RHEL 8, 9 and 10 enterprise lifecycle guidance, subject to the enrolled product, minor stream and subscriptions.

Recheck product and minor release support, extended stream entitlement, supported upgrade path and affected errata classes when deciding to retain or migrate a deployment.

Record subscription and repository evidence, installed stream, applicable errata, expected phase transition and approved upgrade or migration decision.

Scope exclusions include assuming content access, maintenance phase or extended support from the major release alone.

[Red Hat public source](https://access.redhat.com/support/policy/updates/errata)
