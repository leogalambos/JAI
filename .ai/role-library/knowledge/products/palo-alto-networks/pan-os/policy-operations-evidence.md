# Palo Alto Networks NGFW and PAN-OS operations evidence

PAN-OS NGFW managed locally or by supported centralized management. Verify the exact release, model and content compatibility.

Check supported PAN-OS and Panorama versions, associated content, model and subscription before planning rollout or upgrade.

Capture the exact device and policy identity, commit and operational evidence, allowed and denied paths, and the rollback boundary.

Scope exclusions include unapproved traffic policy, unverified subscription capabilities and unrelated cloud security products.
