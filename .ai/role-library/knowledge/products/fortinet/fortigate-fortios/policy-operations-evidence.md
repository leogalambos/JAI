# Fortinet FortiGate and FortiOS policy operations evidence

FortiGate and FortiOS, reviewed against the public 7.6 documentation branch. Verify the model, firmware build and management mode.

Verify the exact model, FortiOS release and upgrade path, managed status and licensed inspection capability before giving release-dependent instructions.

Retain approved policy intent, matching and logging evidence, firmware and management identifiers, and the rollback checkpoint.

Scope exclusions include unapproved traffic policy, unverified hardware and firmware combinations, and unverified FortiCare or FortiGuard entitlement.

[Fortinet public source](https://docs.fortinet.com/document/fortigate/7.6.0/administration-guide)
