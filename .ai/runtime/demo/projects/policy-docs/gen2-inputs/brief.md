# Fictional policy decision brief

ACME Practice Services wants a proposal for reviewing temporary privileged
access to its internal purchasing service. This is a fictional architecture and
policy exercise, not an authorization to inspect or change an actual account.

Propose how a named service owner could approve time-bounded access, separate
requesting and independent review, retain evidence and handle exceptions. No
identity export, production observation, legal assessment or measured SLA is
supplied. Keep these gaps explicit. Planned controls are not observed controls.

The requested result is a justified policy proposal and its current evidence
basis. Actual implementation, customer commitments, human acceptance and release
require their applicable separate decisions; none is granted by this brief.
