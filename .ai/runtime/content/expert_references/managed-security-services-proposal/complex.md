## Managed security service definition and entitlement

Proposed optional service for Orion Payments Group: **Regional 24×7 security operations for payment instruction integrity, fraud/security correlation, privileged change monitoring and settlement evidence anomalies, segmented by legal entity and approved data geography.**. This is a managed detection/triage and escalation service; it does not transfer financial, legal or regulatory decision rights from the client. It does not automatically provide forensic retainer, platform licensing, full remediation or out-of-scope workload onboarding.

Protected estate: channel gateway, jurisdiction policy decision, durable instruction journal, settlement orchestrator, regional connector, parallel ledger, evidence store, three independent failure domains. Monitored data may include payer/payee identifiers, settlement instructions, financial transaction metadata and restricted operational records; access, lawful processing, retention and data location require joint DPA and customer policy review. Service launch is conditional on a signed asset boundary and working telemetry permissions.

## Service hours, measurable SLO, priority and escalation

**Illustrative service level:** 24×7 security alert intake with critical alert triage target within 15 minutes, evidence preservation and escalation; execution of payment-blocking actions, legal notification and finality correction remain under client-controlled dual authority. Measurement starts when a complete, in-scope event is received by the supplier's functioning monitoring platform, not when an external event occurred undetected.

Severity P1: credible active compromise, consequential financial/data-integrity anomaly or critical service control loss; immediate escalation to the authorized customer incident commander. P2: material but contained issue; owner notified within the agreed coverage window. P3: nonurgent finding tracked in monthly improvement.

SLO exclusion candidates require contract approval (unavailable customer source telemetry, planned maintenance, client network blackout); an exclusion cannot hide provider-owned missed alerts. No MTTR or absolute containment guarantee is offered because corrective actions remain customer-authorized.
