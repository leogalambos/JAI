## Managed security service definition and entitlement

Proposed optional service for Meridian Retail Group: **24×7 monitoring of broker integrity, ERP reconciliation exceptions, abnormal identity activity and delivery latency for the two-market pilot, with optional gradual market expansion.**. This is a managed detection/triage and escalation service; it does not transfer financial, legal or regulatory decision rights from the client. It does not automatically provide forensic retainer, platform licensing, full remediation or out-of-scope workload onboarding.

Protected estate: commerce outbox, event broker, ERP adapter, market routing policy, reconciliation store, observability. Monitored data may include order ID, fulfilment events, pseudonymous customer reference and delivery address; access, lawful processing, retention and data location require joint DPA and customer policy review. Service launch is conditional on a signed asset boundary and working telemetry permissions.

## Service hours, measurable SLO, priority and escalation

**Illustrative service level:** 24×7 triage of critical integrity/security alerts within 20 minutes of platform receipt; customer retains incident-command, business-transaction correction and legal notification decisions. Measurement starts when a complete, in-scope event is received by the supplier's functioning monitoring platform, not when an external event occurred undetected.

Severity P1: credible active compromise, consequential financial/data-integrity anomaly or critical service control loss; immediate escalation to the authorized customer incident commander. P2: material but contained issue; owner notified within the agreed coverage window. P3: nonurgent finding tracked in monthly improvement.

SLO exclusion candidates require contract approval (unavailable customer source telemetry, planned maintenance, client network blackout); an exclusion cannot hide provider-owned missed alerts. No MTTR or absolute containment guarantee is offered because corrective actions remain customer-authorized.
