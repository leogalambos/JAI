# Bundled expert reference examples

These 30 English Markdown examples cover ten document families at simple,
medium and complex levels. They are AI-authored proposed professional
illustrations. All organisations, measurements, evidence and execution
results in them are fictional. They are not human-approved standards,
customer evidence, completed tests or JAI L0 authority acts.

The documents retain their supplied bytes, stable reference identifiers,
DCS anchors and provenance. `index.json` records the integrity of these
public-synthetic distributed files. Anchors establish addressability only;
content adequacy, evidence quality and professional acceptance remain
separate questions. The normative DCS registry and existing JAI governance
always take precedence over reference text.

Validate offline with:

```sh
python3 .ai/runtime/tools/expert_references.py validate --root . --all
```

Keep personal, organisation and engagement overrides outside this directory.
See the maintained MkDocs expert reference guide for resolution precedence,
metadata, validation, installation and restoration instructions.
