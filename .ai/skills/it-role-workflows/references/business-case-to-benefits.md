# Business case to realised benefits

`workflow: business-case-to-benefits`  ·  `steps: 7`  ·  `tool-tier: route`

**Trigger.** An investment has a proposed business case and needs measurable benefits governed through and beyond delivery.

**Outcome.** Benefits are baselined, owned, measured after go-live and used for continue/correct/close decisions.

This chain is an enterprise hand-off model, not a rigid project schedule. Run steps in parallel where sensible, but do not skip the acceptance gate between accountable roles.

## Chain

| # | Role | Does | Hands over | Gate before the hand-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Portfolio Manager](../../it-roles-product-delivery-and-agile/references/portfolio-manager.md) | qualifies the investment against strategy, capacity and funding constraints | investment decision context and thresholds | The initiative has an accountable sponsor and an explicit decision threshold |
| 2 | [Business Analyst](../../it-roles-analysis/references/business-analyst.md) | makes the problem, scope, assumptions and outcome measures testable | requirements and outcome baseline | Outcome statements are measurable and sourced |
| 3 | [Benefits Realisation Manager](../../it-roles-product-delivery-and-agile/references/benefits-realisation-manager.md) | turns the case into owned benefits with baselines, targets and dependencies | benefits map, register and realisation plan | No benefit without owner, baseline, target, source and timing |
| 4 | [Product Manager](../../it-roles-product-delivery-and-agile/references/product-manager.md) | aligns product outcomes and roadmap to the benefit hypotheses | roadmap with success measures | Roadmap outcomes trace to registered benefits rather than feature volume |
| 5 | [Organisational Change and Adoption Manager](../../it-roles-product-delivery-and-agile/references/organisational-change-and-adoption-manager.md) | defines adoption dependencies and measures behavioural readiness | adoption plan and readiness measures | Benefit dependencies on behaviour have explicit owners and measures |
| 6 | [Product Analyst](../../it-roles-analysis/references/product-analyst.md) | measures post-go-live outcomes from the agreed sources | observed benefit results and confidence | Results use the pre-agreed method and disclose uncertainty |
| 7 | [Benefits Realisation Manager](../../it-roles-product-delivery-and-agile/references/benefits-realisation-manager.md) | compares actuals to the approved case and recommends action | post-implementation benefits review | Variance, disbenefits and corrective actions are owned and decision-ready |

## Operating this chain

Read the linked role reference before acting as a step and use the operator agent declared for that role's tool tier. A hand-off is complete only when the receiving step applies the stated gate and accepts the evidence. Failed gates remain explicit; they are not converted into assumptions simply to preserve a milestone.
