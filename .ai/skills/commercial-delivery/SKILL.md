# Commercial delivery

> **Agents named here** are subagents: `commercial-coordinator` is a read-only routing adviser; the parent orchestrator dispatches it through the active provider's delegation mechanism.

Add this profile explicitly to the existing engagement; preserve its canonical IDs and technical workflows. Route through the `commercial-coordinator` agent. This skill routes and explains; it does not execute tools, approve a bid, sign a contract or release an artefact.

Read the installed `commercial/catalog.json` compact root first, then selected `commercial/domains/*.json` metadata. Only the selected role loads its own skill and reference. A missing/truncated shard is discovery failure, never proof no specialist exists. The domain router does not replace `model_router.py`: model workload selection and enterprise policy remain a separate handoff.

Commercial work retains the full internal chain from business need and source passage to effort, resources, cost, price and operations. Client projections retain required contractual disclosures while excluding internal rates, margins and confidential rationale unless an explicit disclosure policy requires them. Commercial domain matrix questions are separate from JAI execution layers.

Use the current six SABSA content layers, local setter/author/independent-reviewer
responsibilities, receiving handoffs, bounded rework and human escalation through
the canonical `layered-justifiable-delivery` skill and its
`references/domain-handoff.md` contract. Local entities choose their professionals
and models from the complete catalogue. `/sabsa` library approval alone does not
establish Gen2 justification. Estimate unknowns remain unknown; use discovery,
pilot, stages or alternative contract models instead of fabricating precision.
Apply CTB/RTB or explicitly mapped source taxonomies to both hours and costs.

Cross-domain expertise is requested by the orchestrator using typed consultations, minimal classified inputs, bounded budget/deadline and unchanged contracting owner. Leaves cannot delegate. Response incorporation needs exact-digest independent review and a new canonical dependency version. No consultation grants technical or commercial authority.

For governed readiness, the trusted parent supplies current scoped product,
actual native execution and selected independent review through the shared
`governed_domain.GovernedDomain` callbacks. Retain evidence, disclosure and budget
requirements; metadata or echoed context tokens alone prove no execution.
Unmeasured physical isolation remains explicitly unverified, and a specifically
required security policy must still be satisfied. Existing native sandbox
controls remain authoritative; missing generic hardening attestations do not
disable ordinary native commercial workflows. Only L0 signs reserved decisions,
and neither commercial readiness nor a successful render grants release.

## Mandatory supplier specialisms

Assign each requested responsibility to its dedicated role and load that role's
reference before work begins:

| Responsibility | Required role identifier |
| --- | --- |
| Bid strategy, response compliance and proposal orchestration | `bid-proposal-manager` |
| Selling price, margin, cash flow and deal-finance scenarios | `commercial-pricing-deal-finance-specialist` |
| Basis of estimate, effort/cost uncertainty and calibration | `estimation-cost-engineering-specialist` |
| Independent commercial challenge and assurance findings | `independent-commercial-assurance-reviewer` |
| Supplier commercial lifecycle, negotiation and change economics | `supplier-commercial-deal-manager` |

Do not substitute procurement, TBM, FinOps, pre-sales or resource management for
these scopes. Those roles contribute their own expertise under bounded
consultations. Technical specialists still provide domain estimates; the
estimation specialist owns estimate methodology and aggregation, not invented
technical facts. Decompose requests spanning several specialisms rather than
assigning one broad role. If discovery cannot locate the required specialist,
report a blocking capability gap to the contracting owner.

The assurance reviewer uses the advise tier and an execution identity independent
of proposal, estimate and pricing authors. The reviewer returns findings and
rework criteria, never edits the assessed product, approves a price or accepts
residual risk. Findings return to their author; incompatible scope or risk
escalates through adjacent layers to the human authority. No role grants itself
signature, release or contract approval rights.
