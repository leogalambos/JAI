Add this profile explicitly to the existing engagement; preserve its canonical IDs and technical workflows. Route through the `commercial-coordinator` agent. This skill routes and explains; it does not execute tools, approve a bid, sign a contract or release an artefact.

## Bid work products and their readers

For a complete supplier bid, route the existing work products as a connected
set. Bid / Proposal Manager owns the dossier index, response integration and
version alignment; specialist authors remain accountable for their own evidence
and judgements. A narrow clarification does not require manufacturing an entire
bid pack, but a full offer cannot be represented as supported when its material
internal basis is absent.

| Existing work product or controlled view | Primary readers and use | Required presentation and supporting detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client proposal | Client sponsor, procurement/evaluation team and technical or service acceptance owners deciding whether the offer meets their needs | Explain the proposed outcomes, solution, scope, phased deliverables, acceptance, dependencies, prices and terms in the client's evaluation structure. Retain material qualifications and required disclosures. This is a recipient-ready proposal, not a redacted internal workbook. |
| Internal bid dossier | Delegated bid/price authority, bid team, finance and independent assurance assessing whether the supplier can make the commitment | Provide an actionable recommendation and an indexed, controlled evidence set: technical baseline, phase/work-package plan, basis of estimate, time-phased resource pyramid, financial model, uncertainty and commercial positions. Preserve detailed rationale and calculation sources behind the decision view. |
| Basis of estimate and work/cost breakdown | Technical work owners, estimators, delivery planners and finance reconstructing work and cost | Supply the actual quantities, units, estimation methods, productivity assumptions, effort ranges, source references and allocation basis. Narrative explains the model; it does not replace it. |
| Resource supply/demand and delivery baselines | Resource and line managers, delivery/project leads and affected service owners arranging feasible execution | Show skill and seniority demand by period and work package, effective availability, sourcing, onboarding/supervision, dependencies and headroom. Separate person-effort, allocated capacity and elapsed time; identify unresolved conflicts and receiving owners. |
| Deal financial and cash-flow model and recommendation | Pricing/finance specialists, the delegated commercial decision owner and independent assurance testing economics | Retain formulas, dated cost and sell-rate bases, lifecycle/non-labour costs, reserve treatment, margin/markup, price adjustments and cash timing with scenarios. Give the decision owner a comparison and explicit decision request linked to the full reproducible model. |
| Assurance findings and bid-to-delivery handover | Independent commissioning authority, responsible authors and receiving delivery/service owners acting on deficiencies or accepted commitments | State exact versions, inspected scope, limitations, located findings, required rework and actual receiving-owner dispositions. Handover supplies execution-ready obligations and dependencies; an assurance opinion is not human approval. |

Each individual document or controlled view identifies its intended readers,
their decision or operational task, the baseline/status it describes, owner,
access classification, necessary evidence and next action. Choose its order,
terminology and detail for those readers. A decision brief may lead with a
recommendation; an estimating model must expose calculations; a delivery plan
must support allocation and sequencing. Summaries and annexes must remain
connected and usable without readers reconstructing missing logic or rewriting
generic prose. No universal page limit, stage count or staffing ratio defines
adequacy.

## Connected estimate and commitment baseline

Preserve a reconstructable chain using the engagement's existing identifiers:
source requirement and evidence -> deliverable and acceptance -> phase/work
package and activities/quantities -> skill/seniority and effort -> calendar
capacity and duration -> cost -> reserve/adjustment -> price and cash terms.
Allow justified shared-cost and many-to-many allocations, with allocation bases
and totals that reconcile. A link alone does not validate its quantity, rate or
professional conclusion.

Distinguish evidence-backed estimates, provisional ranges, items requiring
discovery/pilot work and items not yet estimable. Separately identify proposed
commitments and those actually authorised for the exact scope and version.
Derive phase boundaries from the solution and uncertainty. For a learning phase,
state its observation, acceptance, owner and criterion for recalculating or
committing the next phase; never turn unknown later work into a precise price.

Before seeking submission authority, reconcile the same versions across client
and internal views. Independent commercial assurance challenges material
economics and obligations; Resource Manager confirms period-specific capacity
evidence; delivery and relevant service owners assess feasible execution and
accept their receiving obligations. Record acceptance, rejection or unresolved
conditions rather than fabricating sign-off. These specialist dispositions do
not approve the bid or price. Material scope, staffing, schedule, rate or term
changes reopen the affected estimates, capacity/feasibility acceptances,
assurance and reserved human decisions; reuse unaffected evidence only when its
relevant inputs remain unchanged.

## Mandatory supplier specialisms

Assign each requested responsibility to its dedicated role and load that role's
reference before work begins:

| Responsibility | Required role identifier |
| --- | --- |
| Bid strategy, response compliance and proposal orchestration | `bid-proposal-manager` |
| Selling price, margin, cash flow and deal-finance scenarios | `commercial-pricing-deal-finance-specialist` |
| Basis of estimate, effort/cost uncertainty and calibration | `estimation-cost-engineering-specialist` |
| Independent commercial challenge and assurance findings | `independent-commercial-assurance-reviewer` |
| Supplier commercial lifecycle, negotiation and change economics | `supplier-commercial-deal-manager` |

Do not substitute procurement, TBM, FinOps, pre-sales or resource management for
these scopes. Those roles contribute their own expertise under bounded
consultations. Technical specialists still provide domain estimates; the
estimation specialist owns estimate methodology and aggregation, not invented
technical facts. Decompose requests spanning several specialisms rather than
assigning one broad role. If discovery cannot locate the required specialist,
report a blocking capability gap to the contracting owner.

The assurance reviewer uses the advise tier and an execution identity independent
of proposal, estimate and pricing authors. The reviewer returns findings and
rework criteria, never edits the assessed product, approves a price or accepts
residual risk. Findings return to their author; incompatible scope or risk
escalates through adjacent layers to the human authority. No role grants itself
signature, release or contract approval rights.
