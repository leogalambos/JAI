# Layer-local work and review

A question progresses through a setter, an author and an independent reviewer
inside its owning layer. These responsibilities remain distinct even when they
select overlapping professional specialties.

## Assignment and staffing

The setter derives an immutable question assignment from the incoming obligations
and states its criteria, evidence requirements and escalation conditions.
Each entity selects suitable specialties and available model resources from the
complete catalogue. The neighbour does not impose its internal staff or method.

A staffing plan can remain useful for unchanged work. Continuing a mechanical
runtime step does not require repeating an already completed professional choice.

## Candidate and review

The author returns a candidate tied to its exact inputs and assignment. The
reviewer receives a separate context and judges the candidate against stable
criteria. Findings identify the affected requirement and location. The reviewer
must not fix the candidate or silently change the criteria to make it pass.

An inadequate attempt under unchanged requirements receives rework. A defective
local formulation can be revised by its setter with explicit effects. Changing
inherited meaning requires a request to that input's owner.

## Integration and handoff

Reviewed answers form a versioned layer bundle. Independent integration review
checks consistency across questions, shared assumptions, omissions and required
relationships. Six individual positive reviews are not by themselves proof that
the bundle is coherent.

The receiving neighbour accepts an exact handoff for use or returns missing
information and change requests. Handoff acceptance is distinct from professional
review and final human acceptance. See
[the layered operating guide](../using/layered-engagement.md).
