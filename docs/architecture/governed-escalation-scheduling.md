# Escalation, local limits and resumed work

Escalation records unresolved issues and routes them to the adjacent owner able
to decide them. Related conflicts are aggregated because a satisfactory response
can depend on their combined effects.

## Default pause

The affected layer pauses by default: escalation may change shared assumptions.
Completed and late-returning work is retained as history, not automatically
accepted for the revised basis. Unrelated evidence can remain applicable when
its dependencies demonstrably remain unchanged.

An operator may explicitly allow speculative work within a declared scope and
budget to trade token cost for elapsed time. That choice does not accept the
result or waive required review.

## Local bounds

The default question iteration window is ten minutes. Rework is explicit;
after three reworks unresolved disagreement escalates. A timeout reports the
interrupted stage, completed work and reworks used so the operator can grant
additional time or stop. It is not an automatic negative professional judgement
or permission to bypass missing evidence.

These are local divergence controls, not a required aggregate deadline for
every engagement. Extensions do not revive expired grants or reset completed
work invisibly.

## Reconciliation

Resume from the recorded checkpoint and reconcile existing invocation handles
and observations before scheduling another call. New owner decisions explicitly
identify changed inputs, cancelled uses and required fresh work. The runtime
must not inherit reviews or permissions across a changed dependency merely to
avoid an escalation.

A search can report that it found a satisfactory result, was stopped, exceeded
its local allowance or did not find a result within available capacity. Failure
to find a solution is not proof of mathematical infeasibility.
