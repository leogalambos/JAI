# SABSA content and governed views

The [delivery profile](../reference/sabsa-delivery-profile.md) defines required
content for JAI's SABSA-related work. The matrix organises questions; it does not
substitute for the quality or business relevance of their answers.

## Traceable content

Contextual needs lead to conceptual principles and control objectives, logical
services, physical mechanisms and component detail. Management obligations cover
operation, measurement, support and improvement. Every material requirement,
assumption, decision and claim retains a relationship to the inputs that justify it.

Cross-question relationships matter: who operates a control, where it applies and
when it must act must agree with what the control does and why it is required.
A complete set of headings alone does not satisfy those obligations.

## Explicit completeness

Content can be supported, incomplete, contradicted or excluded only where the
applicable profile permits an explicit justified exclusion. A missing required
element is not silently replaced by a plausible-looking paragraph.

Changed inputs require owner-led revisions and fresh affected review.
Completeness and use permission are assessed for the particular output and its
current basis, not inferred from an old report or document title.

## Publication

Governed views reference the accepted semantic records and preserve their
traceability. Audience-specific formatting, rendering and visual quality checks
are separate from professional content review. A valid projection cannot turn
an incorrect source claim into a fact.

The target system's management architecture is also distinct from JAI's own
scheduler. Describing an operational activity does not prove it was executed or
authorise JAI to perform external effects.
