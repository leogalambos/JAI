# Commercial delivery capabilities

The commercial profile supports supplier-side opportunity, discovery, proposal,
delivery economics and service lifecycle work. Begin with
[commercial delivery](commercial-delivery.md) for the user journey and the
[workflow catalogue](../reference/workflows.md) for related enterprise processes.

| Capability | Use and responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Opportunity and discovery | Establish actual client needs, scope, assumptions and evidence before preparing commitments |
| Estimates and learning | Preserve the basis of estimate, uncertainty and actual observations used to revise it |
| Pricing and cash flow | Analyse delivery cost, payment timing, pricing alternatives and explicitly chosen financial assumptions |
| Resource and service planning | Relate demand, skills, capacity, calendars and service obligations without presenting uncertain forecasts as guarantees |
| Commercial change | Trace proposed changes to scope, effort, price, risk and the entitled contractual decision |
| Customer and internal views | Produce audience-appropriate projections while retaining mandatory disclosure and confidentiality boundaries |
| Review and readiness | Obtain independent commercial assurance and accountable human decisions for commitments |
| Monitoring and recovery | Compare actual delivery with the current basis and escalate deviations without inventing approval |

Dedicated specialties include Bid / Proposal Manager, Estimation / Cost Engineering
Specialist, Commercial Pricing / Deal Finance Specialist, Independent Commercial
Assurance Reviewer and Supplier Commercial / Deal Manager. They complement the
[complete role catalogue](../reference/roles.md); they are not a reduced catalogue.

Calculations support decisions but do not certify accounting, legal suitability
or contractual acceptance. A capability description is not evidence that a
specific deployment or client deliverable has been accepted. Continue with the
[commercial guide](commercial-delivery.md).
