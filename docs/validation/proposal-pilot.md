# Proposal colleague pilot

Owner: JAI maintainers · Last reviewed: 2026-10-20

Evaluate a proposal produced from authorized pilot inputs. The bundled
references are AI-authored synthetic examples, not already accepted offers
or a human-approved benchmark. This rubric supports professional feedback;
it does not replace existing commercial, legal, security or JAI authority.

## Record the evaluated version

In the authorized private review record, identify the evaluation, exact
proposal revision, selected kinds and complexity, evaluator role, date,
source-input completeness and sharing/redaction status. Preserve meaningful
integrity evidence privately. Public summaries must omit customer identities,
private source paths, confidential excerpts and correlatable fingerprints.

Score each criterion from 0 to 3: **0** absent or incorrect, **1** weak,
**2** acceptable with changes, **3** professional and evidence-grounded.
Give a precise document location and observation, not just a score.

| ID | Criterion | What the colleague checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Customer understanding | Situation and needs agree with confirmed discovery. |
| E2 | Business justification | Outcomes, value assumptions and success measures support a decision. |
| E3 | Technical soundness | Components, integration, security and failure behaviour are feasible. |
| E4 | Deliverable clarity | Scope, outputs, ownership and SoW acceptance agree. |
| E5 | Delivery credibility | Capacity, dates, dependencies and staged acceptance are achievable. |
| E6 | Commercial integrity | Effort, rates, fees, optional scope, taxes and total costs reconcile. |
| E7 | Security and compliance | Material claims are qualified and traceable to applicable obligations. |
| E8 | RFP integrity | Every applicable question has a truthful response and evidence status. |
| E9 | Client presentation | The proposal is precise, readable, accessible and decision-useful. |
| E10 | Assumptions and risks | Unknowns, confidence, residual risks and gates are explicit. |

## Critical defects override scores

Fabricated customer facts, inconsistent material prices or scope,
unsupported guarantees, prohibited data processing, false certifications,
unapproved authority and missing mandatory requirements are critical defects.
A high aggregate score cannot compensate for them.

For each finding record criterion, score, exact location, verifiable
observation, materiality, owner and corrective action. Record separate
executive, technical, commercial, SoW/legal and presentation dispositions.
The reviewer may recommend `ACCEPT FOR PILOT`, `REVISE` or `REJECT`.
That opinion is separate from contractual, regulatory or product approval.

Include at least one negative case: change a material price, omit a customer
requirement, add an unverified certification or contradict a prerequisite.
The validator should surface the discrepancy or retain an explicit unknown.
It must not turn missing or unexecuted evidence into a confident success.

## Interpret qualification correctly

Offline arithmetic, schema, anchor and cross-document checks establish their
specific deterministic properties. They do not prove professional adequacy,
legal validity, customer acceptance, delivered outcomes or live provider
performance. Human pilot evaluation is optional and separately recorded;
no colleague approval has been fabricated during development qualification.
