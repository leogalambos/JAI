# Glossary

**Acceptance** — An explicit decision establishing a particular output for a
specified use against its governing inputs and criteria. It is distinct from a
reviewer's supported conclusion, permission to continue work and release.

**Applicability** — Whether a record may currently justify the exact output and
scope being assessed. A truthful, intact historical record may be inapplicable
without becoming globally false or corrupt.

**Artifact Contract v3** — A registered composition contract linking an output's
purpose, semantics, justification, design, realisation, evidence and traceability.
Its explicit ID connects the professional output to its content specification.

**Author** — The local entity producing an answer and supporting material against
an issued assignment. It does not independently review its own work.

**Canonical semantic identity** — The stable identity of a domain object or
meaningful work product. An immutable version identifies exact content and
relevant dependencies; a filesystem path only locates material.

**Conditional requirement** — Information required when a stated applicability
condition is true. Unknown applicability is unresolved, not permission to omit
it. Optional information cannot substitute for a required obligation.

**Continuation** — Permission to perform specified further work, including new
verification. It does not accept the resulting output or authorise release.

**Current basis** — The exact governing inputs, applicable evidence and reviews,
and decisions establishing or cancelling justification for a specific output.
It is derived from explicit dependencies and scope, not just the newest timestamp.

**Escalation** — An unresolved matter passed through adjacent ownership to the
entity entitled to change its controlling assumptions or requirements. Related
issues are aggregated; affected work pauses by default. L0 decides changes to
reserved human requirements.

**Evidence** — Attributable supporting material or an observation tied to the
exact inputs and execution it concerns. Its integrity, historical validity,
current applicability and sufficiency are separate questions.

**Fabric** — The append-only Gen2 engagement journal and assessment interface.
It records versions, dependencies, professional work and decisions; it is not a
replacement for canonical domain models or an automatic professional truth judge.

**Gen1 / Gen2** — Gen1 denotes the preceding 26.10.xx release family. Gen2 denotes
26.11.xx, with the revised layered execution and versioned-applicability core.
The useful role, workflow, skill and domain resources remain available.

**Handoff** — Versioned outcomes, requirements and interfaces supplied to a
receiving neighbour or workflow stage. Its receiving acceptance is distinct from
the producer's local review. A neighbour does not prescribe internal staffing.

**Human Authority (L0)** — The external human decision boundary. Only the operator
can sign reserved decisions; a role name, provider approval dialog or chat
assertion is not a signing credential.

**Independent reviewer** — A separate professional entity assessing the exact
candidate against applicable criteria and evidence. It reports findings and does
not repair the work it judges.

**Integrity** — Whether recorded content, identities and supporting material are
intact and available as required. A changed working file does not change the
identity of a preserved immutable input version.

**JAI core** — The Gen2 execution and justification model organised through six
SABSA content perspectives: L1 Contextual, L2 Conceptual, L3 Logical, L4 Physical,
L5 Component and L6 Management. Each addresses What, Why, How, Who, Where and
When. Management concerns also apply across the other perspectives.

**Local triad** — The setter, author and independent reviewer working on a question
inside its owning layer. Each selects suitable professional specialties from the
full catalogue; entity responsibility and professional role are distinct.

**Output content contract / DCS** — The explicit required, conditional, optional
and prohibited information, evidence duties and acceptance criteria for an output,
including its selected shared foundations and overlays. Published proposed rules
are not automatically approved external standards.

**Projection manifest** — The mapping and identity binding from semantic source
content to an audience-specific publication. It distinguishes semantic,
projection and actual rendered-file identities.

**Publication archetype** — An audience/use-specific presentation profile, such
as a Decision Paper or Architecture Review Board Pack. Publication applies when
a rendition is required; executable code and performed work are not made complete
merely by rendering a document.

**Reformulation** — A new question or criterion version published by its entitled
owner because the previous formulation needs to change. It cannot silently
rewrite a neighbour's inputs or a reserved human requirement.

**Release** — Explicit permission for the specified use or distribution of an exact
output. It requires its applicable current justification and any reserved human
decision; a successful render, test or supported review alone does not grant it.

**Rework** — Another answer attempt against the current assignment and criteria.
Its local iteration and retry limits remain recorded; exceeding them leads to
the applicable pause or escalation rather than an automatic reset.

**Scoped cancellation** — An explicit decision removing specified records and
dependent conclusions, reviews or permissions from current use within an affected
scope. History remains intact. Replacement is not automatic verification, and
there is no silent fallback to the cancelled predecessor.

**Setter** — The local entity formulating a question, criteria, evidence duties
and escalation conditions within the incoming layer's requirements.

**Workflow** — A maintained professional sequence connecting roles, inputs,
outputs, receiving conditions and decision boundaries.
