# Estimation / Cost Engineering Specialist: output contracts

Produce and maintain a reproducible basis of estimate for supplier work and lifecycle cost, with calibrated methods, explicit uncertainty and a learning loop from actual delivery rather than unsupported numerical precision.

## Basis of estimate and work/cost breakdown

**At a glance**

- **Purpose:** Basis of estimate and work/cost breakdown
- **Mandatory examples:** Record source versions, extraction time, transformations, lawful access and retention. Evidence completeness, uniqueness, consistency, drift and permitted-use limitations. Define fields, datatypes, units, keys, null handling and allowed values.
- **Conditions to resolve:** personal data are processed; an applicable regulatory requirement exists. Unknown applicability remains unresolved.
- **Optional example:** Include versioned calculations, logs or technical detail only when linked to a specific required claim.
- **Review:** assess substantive content, evidence and gaps against the full selected contract; acceptance and release remain separate.

### Primary work product and evidence

**Primary work product:** `versioned-basis-of-estimate-model`.

Actual work/cost breakdown and estimate model with estimation method, quantities, units, rates, assumptions and uncertainty; narrative accompanies the reproducible model.

**Evidence accompanying the work product:**

- scope/WBS-to-quantity/rate/formula traceability
- reconciliation, calibration evidence, exclusions and estimate confidence range

The historical artifact-type field is retained for compatibility; it does not require replacing this work product with a report or an EA diagram. Content guidance only; existing execution and human authority boundaries remain.
